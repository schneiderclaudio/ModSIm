Model WICY for a water injection cyclone

This model for the hydrocyclone equipped with water injection is based on the work of R. Q. Honaker, A. V. Ozsever, N Singh and B. K. Parekh. Apex water injection for improved hydrocyclone classification efficiency. Minerals Engineering. 14 (2001)1445-1457

The model consists of multilinear correlations for the by-pass fraction, D50c and the imperfection of the classification function. The regression parameters are the spigot diameter, the diameter of the truncated cone, the vortex finder diameter, the injection water rate and the feed pressure and all the quadratic cross products of those parameters.
