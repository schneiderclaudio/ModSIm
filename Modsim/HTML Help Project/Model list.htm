Each model in MODSIM is identified by a four-letter mnemonic. The models are described briefly here and most are described in detail in Modeling and Simulation of Mineral Processing Systems, 2nd edition (2012) by R. P. King and published by the Society for Mining, Metallurgy, and Exploration, Inc., Englewood, Colorado, ISBN 978-0-87335-348-5
