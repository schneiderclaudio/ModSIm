RODM : Rod mill


This model is based on plug flow of the charge through the rod mill. Solids move through the mill in plug flow but the longitudinal transport velocity varies with particle size. Larger particles move more slowly than smaller particles and solids move slower than the water except particles in the last class which move with the water.
