Model CSCR for a vibrating screen

This is the traditional model for screen performance that is based on the capacity of the screen to handle the feed. The capacity factors Are calculated as shown in Modeling and Simulation of Mineral Processing Systems Section 4.1.1

Water sprays can be directed directly to the screen.

Parameters:
1....Mesh size
2....Open area of the screen
3....Angle of inclination
4....Aperture shape
5....Surface condition of the material
6....Surface water retained on the oversize
7....Length of screen
8....Width of screen
9....Number of screens in parallel
