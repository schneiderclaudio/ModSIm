Model DMCY for a dense-medium cyclone

Simulation of the dense-medium cyclone using a modified version of Lynch's equation for the partition curve. The cut point can be defined either by the Gottfried-Jacobsen procedure or the density of the medium can be specified. In the latter case the normalised cut point shift is calculated for each particle size according to the model developed in King R P and Juckes A H "Performance of a dense-medium cyclone when beneficiating fine coal". Coal Preparation 5(1988)185-210. See Modeling and Simulation of Mineral Processing Systems Table 7.1 formula 7

If the Gottfried-Jacobsen method is chosen the variation of cutpoint with particle size is based on the data from the study made by M V Rueda on the operation of coal cleaning equipment in South African collieries.

Corrected imperfection varies with particle size and with the cyclone diameter and is calculated according to the Rueda data or to the King and Juckes model as appropriate.

Parameters
1...Operating density of the medium OR the target cut point.
2...Cyclone diameter.
3...Selector for the model. 1=Gottfried-Jacobsen cut point ratio. 2=Cutpoint shift.
